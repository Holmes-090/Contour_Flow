It interpolates a numeric field of a point layer (for example groundwater elevation, ground elevation, bedrock elevation or survey elevation) with the built-in QGIS TIN or IDW algorithms, or contours an existing raster/DEM directly with GDAL. Options include extent modes, exact boundary clipping, automatic or fixed intervals, major/minor classification, smoothing, labels, style presets and temporary or GeoPackage output.

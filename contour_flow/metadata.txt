description=Generates editable contour lines from point elevations and raster surfaces using native QGIS and GDAL processing tools.
about=Contour Flow is an open-source, single-purpose QGIS plugin: point or raster data in, editable contour lines out. It interpolates a numeric field of a point layer (for example groundwater, ground-surface, bedrock or survey elevations) with QGIS's own TIN Linear, TIN Clough-Tocher or IDW algorithms, or contours an existing raster/DEM with GDAL. It offers automatic or fixed intervals, exact clipping to a boundary polygon, optional smoothing, major/minor contours, labels and style presets, and writes temporary layers or GeoPackage layers (plus an optional GeoTIFF surface). Groundwater Mode turns monitoring wells into groundwater-elevation contours: heads from a field (numeric or numeric text) or calculated as measuring-point elevation minus depth to water, groundwater QA checks (nested wells, heads above ground, off-trend wells, extrapolation), scale-aware smoothing and optional flow-direction arrows derived from the hydraulic gradient of the interpolated surface. All settings are stored in the output layer's metadata. Interpolated contours are estimates derived from the supplied data; results should be reviewed by an appropriately qualified user before technical or professional use. No dependencies beyond QGIS and its Processing plugin.
